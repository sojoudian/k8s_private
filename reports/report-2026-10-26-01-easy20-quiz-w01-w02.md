## Technical Details

Topics, 20 questions:

- containers 2
- images 4
- Dockerfile 5
- ports 3
- CMD, ENTRYPOINT and environment variables 3
- registry and tags 2
- networking 1

The gates:

- GATE 1 to 7 come from `build2.py`. They test the self-contained stem, the
  2026 facts, the length giveaway, the answer leak, the structure, and the
  give-away between two questions.
- GATE 8 balances the key: 5 A, 5 B, 5 C and 5 D.
- GATE 8b limits a run of equal letters to 3.
- GATE 9 holds the strictly longest correct option between 4 and 6 of 20, and
  the strictly shortest between 4 and 6.
- GATE 10 compares every question against the Week 01, Week 02 and Week 03
  pools and the Quiz 3 pool. It rejects a near copy.
- GATE 11 tests the feedback. Each question needs one reason and three
  mistakes, and the text may not name a slide or a week.

Design rules applied to each question:

- Each question tests one concept, and it stands on its own.
- No stem names a slide, a page or a week.
- No stem uses a negative word, so no question says NOT.
- Each question has 4 options and exactly 1 correct option.
- No option says "all of the above", "none of the above" or "both A and B".
- No option set is a pure number list, because the letter balance fixes the
  option order and a number order cannot hold.
